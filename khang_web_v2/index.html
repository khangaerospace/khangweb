About Me

Science, Technology
& Humanity

I am an engineering graduate from University of Toronto, holding a Bachelor of Applied Science in Engineering Science. I design and develop cutting-edge technologies for emerging high-tech markets in Toronto, Canada.

My expertise spans robotics, spacecraft, and aircraft engineering. I currently work as a Satellite Systems Engineering Designer at Kepler Communications, where I help build the internet for space. I also bring industry experience from leading Fortune 500 companies, including Johnson Controls and RTX Corporation.

In my free time, I enjoy mentoring and supporting young professionals as they begin their careers.

Satellite Systems
Space Systems

Building Advance Satellite

I’ve been involved across the full lifecycle of multiple space missions—from early concept development through operations and end-of-life planning. I’ve had the opportunity to learn directly from leading engineers at MDA and UTIAS, strengthening both my technical and systems-level perspective. My experience spans Earth observation missions, such as FINCH with the University of Toronto Aerospace Team, as well as communications constellations like AETHER at Kepler Communications.

Aircraft Engineering

Support Sustainable Aviation

From academic training with professors at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies to hands-on experience in advanced sustainable propulsion systems at Pratt & Whitney Canada, I have contributed to the design and development of advanced aviation systems ranging from UAVs to commercial aircraft engines.
